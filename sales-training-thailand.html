The problem you recognize

Your team learned the method. Does it show up in front of the customer?

Reps come back from sales training energized, with new frameworks and scripts. Then targets, old habits and customer pressure pull them straight back to selling the way they always have.

Pitching, not diagnosingReps talk features and price instead of understanding the customer's real problem.

Discounting under pressureWithout confident negotiation, margin gets given away just to close the deal.

Managers who don't coachSales leaders chase the numbers but rarely coach the behaviors that produce them.

The gap isn't the pitch. It's what your team does in the real conversations that win or lose the deal.
The shift that matters

From product-pushing — to consultative selling customers trust.

Real sales development shows up in the next customer conversation, not the workshop role-play.
