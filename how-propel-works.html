How Propel Works

A structured path from capability gap to performance evidence.

Training is an event. Transformation is a process. Propel supports the whole process — from understanding the real business need to evidence of change at work — through one consistent, structured methodology.

Engagement models

Choose more than a workshop

The methodology can be delivered through three engagement models — on their own or combined.

Model 01

Tailored Training

Practical learning designed around your organization's real business context — awareness, shared language, tools and skill-building.

Model 02

Performance Mentoring

Individual or group support that helps people overcome application barriers and improve real-work performance after training.

Model 03

Capability Development Journey

A structured journey combining assessment, training, application, reinforcement and measurement — from gap to measurable progress.

What you can expect

A partner, not just a provider

Designed around your challenge

Every engagement starts from your business need and the behavior you want to change — never an off-the-shelf course.

Applied beyond the classroom

Learning is reinforced at work through mentoring and follow-up, so new behavior survives real pressure and priorities.

Reviewed for real progress

We measure application, behavior and performance — evidence of change, not just attendance and satisfaction.
