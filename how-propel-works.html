How Propel Works

A structured path from capability gap to performance evidence.

Training is an event. Transformation is a process. Propel supports the whole process — from understanding the real business need to evidence of change at work — through one consistent, five-step methodology.

The methodology

Five steps that turn learning into performance

1

AssessUnderstand the business and capability need

We start by understanding your people, their context and the behavior you need to change — so development targets the real gap, not a generic syllabus.

2

TrainBuild capability through practical, contextual learning

Learning is designed around your real work — building the knowledge, skills and mindset people need, using your own situations and language.

3

ApplyPut new behavior into practice at work

Development continues on the job. People apply what they learned to real tasks and challenges, where new behavior actually takes hold.

4

ReinforceStrengthen change through mentoring and feedback

Structured follow-up and performance mentoring help people overcome the barriers to applying new behavior — so it sticks under real pressure.

5

MeasureReview application, behavior and progress

We look at what changed — whether learning was relevant, capability developed, applied at work, and reflected in behavior and performance.

Engagement models

Choose more than a workshop

The methodology can be delivered through three engagement models — on their own or combined.

Model 01

Tailored Training

Practical learning designed around your organization's real business context — awareness, shared language, tools and skill-building.

Model 02

Performance Mentoring

Individual or group support that helps people overcome application barriers and improve real-work performance after training.

Model 03

Capability Development Journey

A structured journey combining assessment, training, application, reinforcement and measurement — from gap to measurable progress.
