How Propel Works

We don't stop when the workshop ends.

Any provider can deliver a workshop. The real work is everything around it — and how you choose to work with us. Here's what that looks like.

Engagement models

Choose more than a workshop

Propel works with you in one of three ways — on their own, or combined into a single development journey.

Model 01

Tailored Training

Practical learning designed around your organization's real business context — awareness, shared language, tools and skill-building.

Model 02

Performance Mentoring

Individual or group support that helps people overcome application barriers and improve real-work performance after training.

Model 03

Capability Development Journey

A structured journey that combines learning, real-work application, reinforcement and review — built to produce measurable change.

Which approach fits you?

Not sure where to start?

The right engagement depends on where the gap is. A quick guide:

Start with Tailored Training

When a team needs shared language, skills and tools, and you want everyone working from the same playbook.

Add Performance Mentoring

When the knowledge is there but people aren't applying it consistently — the gap is confidence and follow-through, not information.

Choose a full Journey

When a priority capability has to change for real, and you want the whole arc — from the first conversation to evidence of progress.
